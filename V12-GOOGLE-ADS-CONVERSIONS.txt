MK Industrial Solutions V12 – Google Analytics + Google Ads conversions

Primary conversion:
generate_lead

It is fired on tak.html, which is the successful contact-form confirmation page.
This means a conversion is counted only after a contact form has redirected successfully
to the thank-you page.

Secondary event:
cta_click
Tracks clicks on buttons/links whose text contains "Få et tilbud" or "Kontakt".
Do NOT use cta_click as the main Google Ads conversion; generate_lead is the important lead event.

After deployment:
1. Open Google Analytics > Admin > Data display > Events.
2. Submit a real test contact form.
3. Wait a few minutes and confirm generate_lead appears.
4. Mark generate_lead as a Key event.
5. Link Google Ads to this GA4 property.
6. In Google Ads, create/import a conversion based on generate_lead.
7. Set generate_lead as a Primary conversion if you want Smart Bidding to optimize for submitted contact forms.

Important:
Do not test by repeatedly refreshing tak.html, because that can trigger generate_lead again.
Test with an actual form submission.
